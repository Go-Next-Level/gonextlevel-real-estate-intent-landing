## What mortgage intent data represents

Mortgage intent data is evidence that an audience has researched a financing-related topic. Useful categories may include purchase mortgage, refinance, rates, preapproval, home equity, or adjacent home-search activity.

The signal is a prioritization input. It does not establish income, assets, creditworthiness, property ownership, eligibility, protected-class status, consent to contact a particular lender, or a completed application.

## Separate acquisition, retention, and engagement

Mortgage technology is often compared under one label even when systems do different jobs. External intent audiences support net-new acquisition. Customer intelligence monitors or enriches known records. Borrower engagement tools help loan officers educate and convert known opportunities. A lender may need more than one layer.

Define the system role before comparing vendors. Otherwise a platform can look incomplete simply because it was built for another part of the lifecycle.

## Enterprise governance requirements

Before testing, route the intended workflow through legal, compliance, fair-lending, privacy, information security, and marketing-operations review. Document the source categories, permitted use, prohibited uses, identity method, fields, geography, retention, deletion, opt-out, complaint, and audit process.

Customer-side controls matter after export. Restrict seats, log audience movement, minimize retained fields, encrypt transfers, maintain suppression, and prevent the data from being repurposed for eligibility or adverse decisions.

## Build a narrow proof of concept

Choose one product category, market, channel, owner, offer, and evaluation period. Define required fields and exclusion rules. Establish a control cohort and stop criteria. Keep the audience small enough that the team can inspect delivery and consumer feedback.

Measure usable-record rate, contactability, engagement, qualified progression, application starts where appropriate, funded outcomes, cost, complaints, opt-outs, and operational burden. A strong result should be repeatable and explainable.

## Questions for a vendor

Ask how a signal is timestamped; how long delivery takes; whether the identity is person, household, device, or address level; how phones and emails are validated; how opt-outs propagate; what audit logs exist; how sources are licensed; and what happens when a consumer disputes a record.

For an enterprise deployment, request current security evidence, data-processing terms, subprocessor information, incident procedures, retention controls, and a sample file with a data dictionary.

## The operating principle

Use mortgage intent to allocate attention, not to make a credit decision. Validate the facts required for any customer interaction. Keep channel execution inside the lender’s approved systems. Measure incremental results against a control rather than attributing every conversion to the data source.
