## The short definition

Real estate intent data is evidence that a person or household has recently researched a topic connected to a property decision. Examples include home-search activity, seller research, mortgage shopping, home-equity research, or content associated with property distress. The useful output is not a promise that a transaction will happen. It is a time-sensitive clue that helps a team decide where to focus.

That distinction matters. A database can tell you who fits a market. A predictive model can estimate who resembles past movers. Behavioral intent adds evidence about what an audience is researching now.

## Signal, identity, and activation are separate jobs

A responsible evaluation breaks the product into three layers.

1. **Signal detection:** What observable activity qualifies, when did it happen, and how is the category defined?
2. **Identity resolution:** How is an anonymous event associated with a household or contact record, and how is uncertainty handled?
3. **Activation:** Which fields, filters, channels, suppression rules, and permitted uses govern the delivered audience?

A strong result at one layer does not prove quality at another. A recent signal with a poor match is difficult to use. A complete contact record with vague behavioral evidence is simply a list.

## Intent data versus leads

The terms are often blurred in marketing, but they describe different moments. A form-fill lead intentionally enters a workflow. An intent audience is built from behavioral evidence and may not know a particular company. That means outreach, disclosure, suppression, and channel rules require independent review.

Treat an intent record as campaign intelligence—not as proof that the person requested a call.

## What an enterprise buyer should inspect

Ask for the signal definition, source categories, observed timestamp, ingestion delay, refresh cadence, identity methodology, delivered fields, validation schedule, permissible-use language, opt-out process, deletion workflow, and a representative sample.

Then test the data against a fixed brief. Define the geography, behavior, time window, required fields, duplicate rules, outreach channel, owner, control cohort, and outcome before looking at the results. Otherwise it is easy to change the evaluation after the fact.

## How real estate teams use it

Brokerages can use market-level audiences to direct agent prospecting or media. Lenders can separate purchase, refinance, and equity-related research. Loan officers can build a territory-specific pipeline. Investors can prioritize seller and distress research without assuming the signal proves ownership or financial hardship.

In every case, the safest operating principle is the same: use the signal to prioritize, validate the facts required for the workflow, and measure what happens downstream.

## A practical success metric

Do not stop at cost per record. Measure the percentage of records that meet required fields, pass contact validation, survive suppression, enter the intended channel, generate engagement, and produce an agreed business outcome. Include platform, labor, media, and compliance costs.

The best intent-data program is not the one with the largest export. It is the one that produces a repeatable, governed learning loop.
